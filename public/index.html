Amazon to Flipkart Deals Grabber

Amazon to Flipkart Deals Grabber is a free, independent tracker that shows live discounts from Amazon.in and Flipkart on one page. It reads public deal and search listings for phones, laptops, tablets, earbuds, smart watches, televisions and cameras, then ranks them by how much the price has dropped. There is no account, no app and no middleman: every card links straight to the marketplace listing.

How the deal feed works

  • Updated every 10 minutes. A scheduled job re-reads both stores and republishes the snapshot.
  • New deals appear automatically. Anything the crawl has not seen before is tagged New for its first day.
  • Expired deals drop off. Listings that leave the stores age out, so you are not sent to a dead page.
  • Filter and search. Narrow by store (Amazon or Flipkart), by category, or by typing a product name.

Frequently asked questions

Is Amazon to Flipkart Deals Grabber affiliated with Amazon or Flipkart?
No. It is an independent shortcut that only reads publicly visible listings and links back to the stores. It takes part in the Amazon Associates programme, so some links are affiliate links and we may earn a commission from qualifying purchases — at no extra cost to you, and it never changes the price you pay.
Are the prices guaranteed?
No. Prices, stock and offers change without notice, so always confirm the final amount at checkout on Amazon.in or Flipkart.
How often are deals refreshed?
Roughly every 10 minutes, and the page also refreshes itself while it is open. You can press “Refresh now” at any time.
